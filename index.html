      How it's calculated & settings

      Each meal's offset is the welfare gap: the extra you would have paid if the meat had come from animals raised and slaughtered to high welfare standards (e.g. slower-growing breeds, outdoor or pasture access, lower stocking densities, effective stunning).

      offset = price paid × meat share × (humane multiplier − 1)

      Meat share is roughly how much of the price was the meat itself. A restaurant plate's price mostly covers labour, rent and sides; a grocery purchase is nearly all meat. Humane multiplier is roughly how many times more the high-welfare version costs than typical conventional meat. The defaults below are rough estimates based on typical US retail price gaps between conventional and pasture-raised / higher-welfare-certified products. Adjust them to match your own prices. Changes apply to new entries only; logged meals keep the numbers they were logged with.
